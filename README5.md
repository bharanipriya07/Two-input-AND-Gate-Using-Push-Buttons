#  HIGH VOLTAGE

**Description**  
A simple LED switching circuit assembled on a perfboard using two push buttons. The buttons control the ON/OFF operation of the LED.

**Components Used**  
Push Button – 2  
LED – 1  
Resistor – 1  
Battery/DC Power Supply – 1  
Connecting Wires  
Perfboard

**Procedure**  
Place the push buttons, LED and resistor on the perfboard.  
Connect the components according to the circuit.  
Connect the battery/DC supply using wires.  
Check all connections.  
Press the push buttons and observe the LED.

**Working**  
When the required push button(s) are pressed, the circuit is completed and current flows through the LED, making it glow. When the switch is released, the circuit is interrupted and the LED turns OFF.
